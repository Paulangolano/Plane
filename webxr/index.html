Quest Touch controllers

  • Right grip grab the flight stick (or use the right thumbstick)
  • Left grip grab the throttle and push it forward (or left thumbstick up)
  • Left thumbstick ←→ rudder
  • Right trigger afterburner, left trigger brakes
  • A restart, B / X comfort vignette, Y mute
  • Click the left thumbstick to re-centre your seat

Keyboard

  • W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E rudder
  • Shift / Ctrl throttle up / down
  • Space afterburner, B brakes
  • V chase camera, Enter restart
  • Drag the mouse to look around

Quest Touch controllers

  • Left grip hold the side yoke: pull back = nose up, sideways = roll. Its thumbstick is the trim hat
  • Right grip hold the power lever and push it forward
  • Or fly with the right thumbstick; left thumbstick = power ↑↓ and rudder ←→
  • Triggers left / right toe brakes (steer on the ground)
  • A / B flaps down / up, X autopilot on/off
  • Touch the panel buttons with a fingertip: AP, HDG, ALT, VS, LVL, flaps, park brake…
  • Emergency: grab and pull the red overhead parachute handle, or hold the overhead Safe Return button
  • Hold Y restart, click the left thumbstick to re-centre

Keyboard

  • W/S A/D Q/E pitch, roll, rudder · Shift/Ctrl power
  • [ / ] flaps up / down · T / G trim nose down / up
  • B brakes · , / . left / right brake · P parking brake
  • Z autopilot · H HDG · K ALT · U VS · L LVL
  • - / = heading bug · PgUp/PgDn altitude · J/N VS
  • Hold 9 parachute · hold 0 Safe Return · O assists
  • V chase camera · Enter restart
