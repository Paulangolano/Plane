Quest Touch controllers

  • Right grip grab the flight stick (or use the right thumbstick)
  • Left grip grab the throttle and push it forward (or left thumbstick up)
  • Left thumbstick ←→ rudder
  • Right trigger afterburner, left trigger brakes
  • A restart, B / X comfort vignette, Y mute
  • Click the left thumbstick to re-centre your seat

Keyboard

  • W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E rudder
  • Shift / Ctrl throttle up / down
  • Space afterburner, B brakes
  • V chase camera, Enter restart
  • Drag the mouse to look around
